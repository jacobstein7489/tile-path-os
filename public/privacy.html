Privacy policy

Tile OS — Cobblestone Inc. · Last updated October 29, 2026

Tile OS is the private operating system Cobblestone Inc. uses to run its own tile installation projects. Only people Cobblestone lets in can sign in to it. It is not offered to the public or to other companies.

What it keeps

The company's own work: projects, estimates, schedules, purchase orders, invoices, payments, and the names, phone numbers and email addresses of the company's team, clients, suppliers and installers.

QuickBooks Online

When Cobblestone connects its QuickBooks Online company, Tile OS reads and writes only what it needs for the company's own billing: customers, products and services, invoices and payments. The QuickBooks sign-in tokens are kept on Tile OS's server, never in a browser, and are deleted when the company disconnects QuickBooks.

Who sees it

Only Cobblestone's team, each person seeing the part of the work their login allows. Nothing is sold, rented or shared with anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.

Where it lives

On Tile OS's hosting and database providers, in the United States, protected by sign-in and access rules.

Removing data

Cobblestone's administrator can delete projects, people and the QuickBooks connection at any time from inside Tile OS. For anything else, contact Cobblestone Inc. directly.
